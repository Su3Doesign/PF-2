The Flooded Rotunda

Launch live site ↗ (opens in a new tab) See it on the voyage All work

Twelve flooded arches and a marble keeper who turns to face you as the world loads.

A drowned Romanesque ring of twelve bays, 7.9 million triangles of unique geometry plus 1.56 billion instanced through Nanite. The place has one rule: she turns to face whoever comes in. While the world downloads she is the loading screen, drawn in chalk, looking for you.
