After-hours food and dreamwear, drawn from one hypnogram. Open 19:00 to 04:00.

Over a lifetime you spend about six years dreaming and remember almost none of it. REM is where that time goes. The whole system comes from the sleep hypnogram: five stage rows joined only by right-angle steps. Dark until dreaming: four neutrals carry 92% of every surface, and Lucid pink appears only where something is dreaming.

Concept project. Fictional brand, no affiliation.
