A car magazine you read by scrolling: five issues, five cars in real-time 3D.

Ferrari 812 Superfast, Koenigsegg Agera, Bugatti Chiron, Koenigsegg One:1 and Mercedes-AMG Project One, each painted, lit and set up in Blender. Press start on the rev counter and the engine fires with a sound I designed from scratch, with no recordings. Turn on the headlights, pick new rims, and drive a rolling road until the brakes glow.
