Hyperion GEN.2

Launch live site ↗ (opens in a new tab) See it on the voyage All work

A hypercar I designed and modelled from scratch, with a live 3D site.

I wanted Hyperion to feel like a real car, so it has 20 rigged assemblies with working hinges, doors that swing up 100° and a 22-part V8. I designed the logo and carried it onto the tyres, brakes, cabin labels and dashboard screen. On the site you sweep a light over the car, spin it 360°, take it apart, and end on a meadow of 72,000 moving blades of grass.
