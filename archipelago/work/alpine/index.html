Alpine F1 Study

Launch live site ↗ (opens in a new tab) See it on the voyage All work

My own F1 car in Alpine livery: 23 rigged assemblies, a wind tunnel and an exploded view.

I set the car up in Blender as 23 rigged assemblies with steering and DRS hinges, baked the livery into 4K textures, designed Pirelli P Zero sidewalls and modelled a 1.6 L V6 hybrid power unit in 21 labelled parts. On the site smoke flows over the car in a wind tunnel, it comes apart in an exploded view, and the night ends in a pit lane.
