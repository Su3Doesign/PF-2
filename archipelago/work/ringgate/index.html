The Ring Gate

Launch live site ↗ (opens in a new tab) See it on the voyage All work

A 156-metre stone ring in the sea and the drowned city below it, as a scrolling art book.

Kaalavalaya is a carved stone ring with a door that shows you what you want most and takes your shadow in return. Tirthavel is the drowned city climbing toward it. I designed the world, wrote its story, modelled it in Blender, rendered it in Unreal and built the book on the real gate mesh. One day passes over the Ring while you read.
