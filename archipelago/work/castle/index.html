The Castle on the Crag

🔒 Live site coming soon See it on the voyage All work

Forty-five towers on two granite crags above a Highland loch, as one 3D world.

A viaduct, the boathouse stairs, a stadium and 644,000 trees, moss cushions and boulders. I modelled 120 meshes myself, planned 17 cameras and lit the world five ways: night, day, sunset, dusk and mist.
