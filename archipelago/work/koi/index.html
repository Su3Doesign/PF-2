Koi Pond

🔒 Live site coming soon See it on the voyage All work

A garden pond where the water is the light source, in summer and in autumn.

The rule for this one: no light from above. Everything is lit from inside the water, so the caustics run the wrong way up the rocks and the leaves get their colour from below.
