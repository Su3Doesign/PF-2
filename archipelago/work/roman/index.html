A pagoda at first light, lit again for pink hour, a crescent moon, a full moon and an eclipse.

The hour before anything is awake. I wanted the fog to do the modelling: the pagoda barely earns its silhouette and the only hard edge in the frame is the sun clearing the ridge. Roughly four hundred rejected sun angles.
