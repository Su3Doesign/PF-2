Altair Estates

Launch live site ↗ (opens in a new tab) See it on the voyage All work

A cliff estate you drive through: 808 trees, 2,141 lamps, 52 cameras.

I drew the plans first: a gate, a maple avenue between a lake and a lavender field, a glass-block bridge, a gallery street, a palm canal and a house cut into a sea cliff. The site arrives as a pencil drawing and turns into the white model, then the real render develops over it as you drive.
