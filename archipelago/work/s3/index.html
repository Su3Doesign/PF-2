My studio mark: world, web and brand. Three things that shouldn't connect, made to hold.

S—3 is the mark I work under: an impossible triangle whose three bars are world, web and brand. They shouldn't connect, and the job is making them hold, drawn so it looks real. The social system runs on one line, Built in the dark. Lit with intent.: chapter cards, signals, teardowns and an archive, all on a near-black grid with one acid accent.
