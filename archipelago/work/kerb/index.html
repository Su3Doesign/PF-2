KERB

🔒 Live site coming soon See it on the voyage All work

A motorsport coffee roaster: every cup is a lap, every blend a corner.

Blends are named after the corners of a fictional circuit: Hairpin espresso 01, Apex house blend 07, Chicane decaf 12, Slipstream cold brew. The wordmark is sheared to 12° over a ten-block kerb strip, and that angle drives the badge, the livery and every graphic. Every bag carries a lap sheet with lot, drop temperature and a roast trace coloured by sector.

Concept project. Fictional brand, no affiliation.
